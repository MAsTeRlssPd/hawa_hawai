KNOW WHAT YOU’RE SEEING

Data and privacy

Monitor readings and model forecasts are shown separately. Saans currently shows PM2.5 concentration, not an official CPCB National AQI. CPCB AQI needs averaged readings and pollutant sub-indices. Wind is live from Open-Meteo; the smoke corridor and fire visuals are illustrative.
